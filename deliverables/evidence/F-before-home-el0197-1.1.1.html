The image 'morearrow.gif' is the sole content of a link navigating to 'news.html'. Its alt attribute is explicitly empty (""), rendering the link nameless. A screen reader user receives no information about the link's purpose. The scanner confirms: 'Img element is the only content of the link, but is missing alt text. No honest case exists. The image is the sole content of the link, and its alt text is empty (""), meaning assistive technology users receive no information about the link's purpose or destination. The scanner correctly identifies this failure. The image is not decorative — it is a functional navigation control linking to news.html.

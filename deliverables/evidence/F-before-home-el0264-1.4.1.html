Case for a failure

The link 'Read More...' appears inline within running text and is distinguished from surrounding content only by color. The inline style explicitly removes underlines: 'text-decoration:none;'. The scanner confirms no other visual cue exists on normal, hover, or focus states. WCAG 1.4.1 prohibits color as the sole visual means of distinguishing a visual element. This is a textbook failure of that criterion.

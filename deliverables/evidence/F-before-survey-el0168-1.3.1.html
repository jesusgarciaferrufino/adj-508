Case for a failure

The radio buttons are not programmatically associated with their answer labels. Each radio input sits in a separate nested table column from its corresponding text label ('None', 'Central Park', 'Grand Park', etc.), with no label element, aria-labelledby, or aria-label linking them. The relationship between each radio button and its option text is conveyed purely through visual positioning, not through any programmatic structure, violating 1.3.1.

Case against a failure

The radio buttons lack programmatically associated labels and are not grouped with a fieldset/legend to associate them with their answer options. Each radio input is in a separate layout table column from its corresponding text label ('None', 'Central Park', 'Grand Park', etc.), with no label element, aria-label, or aria-labelledby connecting them. The relationship between each radio button and its answer option cannot be programmatically determined.
